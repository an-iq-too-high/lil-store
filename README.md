# Options
## Buy
to open this option, either say “```buy```” or “```1```” to activate, there’s currently 5 items.
## Sell
to open this option, either say “```sell```” or “```2```” to activate, in order to sell, you must already buy/have the item in your inventory.
## Inventory
to open this option, either say “```inventory```” or “```3```” to activate, this will tell you what stuff you have.
## Transactions
to open this option, either say “```transactions```” or “```4```” to activate, this will tell you what you made over the time, this uses saving.
## Money
to open this option, either say “```money```” or “```5```” to activate, this will simply tell you how much $$$ you have.
## Help
to open this option, either say “```help```” or “```6```” to activate, this will just tell you the commands.
## Exit
to open this option, either say “```exit```” or “```7```” to activate, this will exit the program.
## Purchases
to open this option, either say “```purchases```” or “```8```” to activate, this will show you the total amount of things you bought in one session.
## Sells
To open this option, either say “```sells```” or “```9```” to activate, same as above, just stuff you sold.
## Secrets
this option is a secret, only for non-coders, anyone who knows how to code will already know it.
